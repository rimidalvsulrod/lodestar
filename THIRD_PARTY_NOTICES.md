# Third-party notices

## Design reference

The 1.1 interface layout (full-bleed globe in a framed viewport, floating glass panels, HUD readouts, bottom command dock, visual presets) is modelled on **God's Eye View** by Bilawal Sidhu, https://github.com/bilawalsidhu/gods-eye-view, released under the MIT License (Copyright (c) 2026 Bilawal Sidhu). No God's Eye View source files or third-party datasets are included in Lodestar.

## Libraries and assets

- globe.gl and three-globe (MIT), including the earth, topology and night-sky textures from the three-globe examples
- d3 (ISC), topojson-client (ISC), world-atlas (ISC, data from Natural Earth, public domain)
- TradingView Lightweight Charts (Apache 2.0)

USGS earthquakes, NOAA National Hurricane Center, NASA EONET, NASA GIBS imagery, GDACS, public news RSS feeds (BBC, Al Jazeera, The Guardian, The New York Times, DW, France 24, NPR, UN News, OilPrice) and Yahoo Finance delayed quotes. Each remains subject to its provider's terms.
